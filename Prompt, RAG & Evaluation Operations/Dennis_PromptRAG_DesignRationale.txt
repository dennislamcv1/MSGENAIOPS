Design Rationale - Dennis_PromptRAG_Framework (Contoso Health System Clinical Knowledge Assistant)

My top design choice was to let the clinical risk tier drive every downstream control, with a "highest tier wins" rule so that any change touching drug-interaction content (including a shared embedding model or a patient leaflet that mentions an interaction) is governed as Critical. For that tier I gate on groundedness of at least 4.6 on every individual response, not just the run average, and add a zero-tolerance, pharmacy-SME-curated drug-interaction evaluator, because Foundry's built-in groundedness only proves an answer matches its retrieved source and a stale monograph can still score perfectly. I also moved shadow mode after pharmacist sign-off for Critical changes, since the scenario brief counts shadow traffic as production exposure.
