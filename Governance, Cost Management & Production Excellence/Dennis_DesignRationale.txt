As GLC's GenAIOps lead, my most important design decision was to route every model call and every agent action through one logically centralized but regionally deployed policy enforcement plane (an Azure API Management AI gateway plus a separate, deterministic Action Policy Service), so that EU and US personal data stays in its Data Zone, Brazil and India use regional deployments, and Global processing is limited to GLC-NetAssist's non-personal operational data. GLC-ExceptionAgent can only propose: it auto-executes service credits up to USD 250 and reroutes up to USD 1,000 only when every policy condition passes and the customer has received no more than USD 500 in auto-credits in 30 days, while larger credits need operations-manager or dual Finance and Legal approval, and customs, sanctions, dangerous-goods and personal-data decisions stay human-only. I chose this because a content-filter result is not permission to act, and separating decision authority from the model keeps unauthorized actions, cross-border exposure and missing evidence under control however the agent is finally classified under the EU AI Act.
