My top design choice is one shared GenAIOps pipeline and Azure API Management AI gateway that apply risk-tiered controls per app, instead of three separate platforms. Sharing build, versioning and gateway policies removes duplicated infrastructure and enforces per-app token quotas for CFO showback, while switching traffic at the gateway contains operational risk by keeping rollback within 3-5 minutes. Automated numeric gates let routine releases ship twice weekly, with human approvals and longer observation added only where risk demands it, chiefly for HRHelper.
